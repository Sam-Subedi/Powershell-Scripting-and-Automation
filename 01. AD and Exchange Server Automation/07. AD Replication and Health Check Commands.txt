dcdiag: Runs a general Domain Controller diagnostic. Checks common AD/DNS/replication and DC health issues.

dcdiag /v: Runs dcdiag in verbose mode, giving much more detailed diagnostic information. Useful when troubleshooting a problem.

dcdiag /test:dns: Specifically tests DNS health on the Domain Controller. Checks DNS configuration, DNS registration, and AD-related DNS records.

dcdiag /test:replications: Tests Active Directory replication between Domain Controllers and looks for replication-related problems.

repadmin /replsummary: Gives a summary of AD replication health across Domain Controllers. Helps identify Domain Controllers with replication failures or errors.

repadmin /showrepl: Shows detailed inbound replication status for the Domain Controller, including which Domain Controllers it replicates from and whether replication succeeded or failed.

net share: Displays the shared folders and resources currently shared by the server.

net share SYSVOL: Checks and displays the SYSVOL share. SYSVOL contains important domain files such as Group Policy and logon-related files.

net share NETLOGON: Checks and displays the NETLOGON share. NETLOGON is used by Domain Controllers for logon scripts and other domain authentication-related files.


# Real World Use:

dcdiag: Runs a general DC health check (AD, DNS, services, connectivity). Real-world use: Your first line of defense when users suddenly can't log in or Group Policy fails to apply.

dcdiag /v: Runs the diagnostic in verbose mode for deeper test results and warnings. Real-world use: Used when a standard dcdiag reports a failure but doesn't give you enough detail to find the root cause.

 dcdiag /test:dns: Specifically checks DNS configurations, SRV registrations, and zone records. Real-world use: Crucial when workstations are failing to find a Domain Controller on the network.

 dcdiag /test:replications: Targets AD database replication health between DCs. Real-world use: Used when changes (like a new user or password update) made on one DC aren't showing up on another.

repadmin /replsummary: Provides a high-level summary of replication health across all DCs. Real-world use: A quick way to spot exactly which Domain Controllers are failing replication without checking each one individually.

repadmin /showrepl: Displays detailed inbound replication data, partners, and error codes. Real-world use: Once replsummary flags an error, you run this to find out exactly which replication partner is failing and when it last worked.

net share: Lists all shared resources on the server. Real-world use: Run immediately after a DC reboot to quickly confirm that the server is actually sharing out its critical identity folders.

 net share SYSVOL: Verifies the local policy share exists. This holds GPOs, templates, and scripts (replicated via modern DFSR). Real-world use: If users aren't receiving updated Group Policies, you run this to confirm the share is alive before diving into background DFSR troubleshooting.

net share NETLOGON: Verifies the log on share exists (maps to SYSVOL\domain\scripts for legacy scripts). Real-world use: Its presence is a vital indicator that the DC is ready to handle authentication traffic, though overall health must still be verified via dcdiag.

Use net share for quick share availability, repadmin for database sync issues, and dcdiag for comprehensive structural health.

Summary:
dcdiag → Is my DC healthy?
dcdiag /v → Give me detailed health information
dcdiag /test:dns → Is DNS working correctly?
dcdiag /test:replications → Is AD replication working?
repadmin /replsummary → Which DCs have replication problems?
repadmin /showrepl → Show me the detailed replication relationships/errors
net share → What shares exist?
SYSVOL → Is the SYSVOL share present?
NETLOGON → Is the NETLOGON share present?
